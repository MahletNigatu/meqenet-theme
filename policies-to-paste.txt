===============================================================================
RETURN AND REFUND POLICY
===============================================================================

Every MEQENET piece is printed and sewn after you order it. Nothing is held in
stock, which means a returned garment cannot be resold. Our policy is built
around that.

Damaged, defective or misprinted. If your order arrives damaged, misprinted, or
materially different from what was shown, write to us within 30 days of
delivery with a photograph of the item and of the packing slip. We will remake
and reship it at no cost, or refund it in full, whichever you prefer. You do
not need to send the original back.

Lost in transit. If tracking shows no movement for ten business days, or shows
delivery to an address you did not enter, contact us and we will remake the
order.

Wrong size ordered. Because the piece was made to your selection, a size change
is not a defect. Contact us within 30 days and we will work something out in
good faith, though we cannot promise a free replacement. The size guide on the
Contact page is the best way to avoid this.

Change of mind. Email us within two hours of ordering and we can usually cancel
before production begins. Once production has started the piece exists only for
you and cannot be cancelled.

Incorrect address. If a parcel returns to us because the address was wrong or
incomplete, we will reship once you confirm the correct address. Reshipping is
charged at cost.

How refunds are issued. Approved refunds return to the original payment method.
Your bank or card issuer controls the timing, typically five to ten business
days after we process it.

Sale items and gift cards. Sale items follow this same policy. Gift cards are
not refundable.

How to start. Use the form on our Contact page, or reply to your order
confirmation email. Include your order number.

SHIPPING POLICY
===============================================================================

Production time. Two to five business days for printed apparel and accessories.
Embroidered items can take one to two days longer. Production time is separate
from transit time, and orders placed on a weekend begin production the next
business day.

Transit time. Domestic orders typically arrive three to five business days after
shipping. International orders vary widely by destination, commonly ten to
twenty business days.

Rates. Shipping is calculated at checkout from your address and the contents of
your order. The amount shown at checkout is the amount charged.

Tracking. A tracking link is emailed the moment your order ships. Carriers can
take 24 to 48 hours to show the first scan; a link that looks inactive at first
is normal.

Split shipments. Orders containing several item types may be produced in
different facilities and arrive separately, at no extra charge to you.

Customs, duties and import taxes. International orders may attract import duties
and taxes set by the destination country and collected by the carrier on
delivery. These charges are not ours, are not included in your order total, and
cannot be refunded by us. Refused parcels returning to us are treated as an
incorrect-address case.

Where we ship. Anywhere our checkout accepts an address. If your country does
not appear at checkout, we cannot currently ship there.

Delays. Weather, customs inspections and peak-season carrier volume can add time
beyond these estimates. We will tell you what we know if you ask.
